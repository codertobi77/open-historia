Open Historia Guides

Everything you need to play, host, and master Open Historia — the free open-source grand strategy game that runs in your browser.

Get Started

Start playing Open Historia right in your browser — no install, no account, nothing to download. Learn the main menu, how to start a game, and where to find community scenarios.

How to Play

Learn the core mechanics: interactive world map, AI diplomacy via natural language chat, troop deployment and combat, AI-generated events, AI strategic advisor, the vector map editor, and the Scenario Hub. Tips for new players and a recommended gameplay loop.

AI Setup Guide

Connect a local large language model (LLM) to unlock AI-powered diplomacy, AI-generated historical events, and the AI strategic advisor. Covers Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, and OpenAI-compatible cloud APIs. Includes model recommendations and troubleshooting.

Self-Hosting Guide

Host your own copy of the Open Historia web build — for offline use, your LAN, or to run an AI backend for friends. Covers building the site, serving it with a static host, and the AI backend.

Pax Historia Alternative

How Open Historia compares to Pax Historia and Age of History 3. Full feature comparison table, pricing breakdown, platform support, and answers to common questions about switching from commercial grand strategy games.
